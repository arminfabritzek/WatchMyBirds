Your birds could teach the next station

Every bird your station recognises, it recognises because someone else sat down and corrected it first. If you ever feel like passing that on, the exports contain the answered photos you could send.

There is no hurry and no obligation. Download it, look through it, keep it on your own machine as long as you like. Nothing leaves this device unless you send it yourself.

If you decide to share it, please don't attach the archive to a public issue — it holds your images. Say hello first and we'll find a sensible way to hand it over.
